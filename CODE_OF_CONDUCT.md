# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Bowerbird respectful and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experience levels, viewpoints, and boundaries.
- Protect private information, credentials, and personal knowledge-base data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in Bowerbird project spaces and when someone publicly
represents the project. It covers issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not use a public issue to report conduct that exposes sensitive or personal
information. For conduct on GitHub, use GitHub's private **Report content**
feature from the relevant comment, issue, pull request, or profile. GitHub
documents that process at:

https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily ban,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially
as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this conduct
reporting process.
